About

ExSoCal returns in 2027 at UNLV. ExSoCal 2027 will bring together scientists from Southern California and Nevada who work in exoplanets over three days in February. Join us in Las Vegas for a few days of science and collaboration.

The conference will start at 1pm on Thursday, February 4 and will finish at noon on Saturday, February 6, 2027.
